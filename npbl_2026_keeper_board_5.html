NPBL keeper rules — as of 2026

This is the current ruleset, effective for the 2026 season and superseding the 2024 & Beyond rules. Rules 1–3 and 5–8 carry over unchanged. Rule 6 has been repealed. Rules 8, 9 and 10 are new.

  1. Three keepers, three seasonsKeep up to 3 players. A player can be kept a maximum of 3 seasons before he must go back into the draft pool. Trades do not reset the clock.
  2. Cost is draft round minus 2A player drafted in round 7 costs you a round 5 pick.
  3. No first or second roundersAnyone drafted in rounds 1 or 2 cannot be kept. That follows the player all season regardless of who ends up rostering him.
  4. The current owner sets the priceClaimed off waivers or free agency? The FAAB table applies and his draft round is ignored. Drafted him yourself, or got him in a trade? Round minus 2. A player who changes hands is repriced by whoever holds him now.
  5. FAAB pricing$0 → R11 · $1–3 → R10 · $4–6 → R9 · $7–9 → R8 · $10–12 → R7 · $13–15 → R6 · $16–19 → R5 · $20+ → R4. Budget is $100.
  6. Pick tradingrepealedDraft picks are no longer tradeable — players only. Nobody traded a pick in 2025, and the old rule blocking a keeper whose round you'd dealt away goes with it. There are no traded picks left to track.
  7. PlayoffsWeeks 15–17, top 6 teams, top 2 seeds get a week 15 bye.
  8. One keeper per roundnew for 2026 Every keeper costs a different round. If two of your guys both price at an 11th you can only keep one of them — no sliding to the next open pick. Applies the same whether you drafted him in the 13th or claimed him off waivers.
  9. The champion sets the draft ordernew for 2026 Last season's winner decides the order for the coming draft. For 2026 that's Marty.
  10. No keepers from the dead weeksnew for 2026 A player picked up after the final regular-season week carries no keeper rights that year. Roster moves stay open to everyone — you just can't turn a December claim into next year's keeper.
  11. Dues and payouts$250 to play. 1st takes $1,750, 2nd takes $500, most points scored takes $250, plus a split of any fines collected.
  12. Fines$50 for starting an inactive player. $25 a week from the low scorer to the high scorer.

Why rule 10 exists

Rule 10

Four days in December

The Josh Allen window

Nobody broke a rule here — there wasn't one to break, and there was no money on the table. But the sequence shows how much keeper value the dead weeks were quietly handing out, and it would have happened again every December.

  • Dec 23 – 29Week 17. Charlie — the number one seed — is playing Deegan in the third-place game, having lost the semifinal to Forde by three points.
  • Dec 25, 2:28 amCharlie drops Josh Allen mid-game. His own round-3 pick and his starting quarterback. Travis Kelce goes with him.
  • Dec 25, 2:29 pmHe adds Quinn Ewers, Audric Estime, then Philip Rivers — retired since 2020 — and Justin Tucker.
  • Dec 28, 3:32 amTommy claims Allen for $51. Tommy finished 7th and missed the playoffs. He'd banked FAAB all season — his biggest previous bid was $22.
  • Dec 29Charlie loses 95.18 to 138.64, the lowest score posted by anyone in the bracket. Josh Allen is now a 4th-round keeper for a team that never made the playoffs.

FAAB remaining when Allen hit the wire. Tommy was the only owner who could reach $51 — Forde was a dollar short.

A lock on non-playoff teams wouldn't have fixed this: Allen still leaves Charlie's roster, and Forde takes him instead at $50 — moving a 4th-round Josh Allen to the runner-up. And of the 22 keepers picked up after week 14, 21 went to playoff teams. Marty was adding Kirk Cousins on Dec 26 while winning the title.

Worth saying plainly: third place pays nothing in this league, and Charlie had already locked the $283 points-for prize back in week 14 — playoff points don't count toward it. He had no money riding on that game. Nobody is accusing anybody of anything.

Rule 10 removes the prize instead of restricting the players. Twenty-two names come off the board — eighteen of them defenses, kickers and retirees nobody would keep anyway. The four that sting: Josh Allen, Aaron Jones, Darren Waller, Dalton Kincaid.
